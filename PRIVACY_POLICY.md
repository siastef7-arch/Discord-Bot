## What the bot collects

Rift Quests stores your Discord user and server IDs; linked Riot ID, PUUID, and region; selected position; quest progress; earned ranks; processed match IDs; and operational records such as command cooldowns and audit events. Server configuration includes role and announcement channel IDs.

The bot retrieves match data from Riot to check quest requirements. Match responses can include other participants. Full match responses are processed temporarily rather than saved in the bot database.

The bot uses slash commands. It does not read ordinary message content or request your Discord or Riot password.

## How information is used and shared

Information is used to link accounts, verify quest progress, prevent duplicate match credit, manage Discord roles, and operate the bot. Discord and Riot receive the requests necessary for these features. The operator and hosting providers may access stored data to operate and maintain the service.

Ranks and position roles are visible in your server. The leaderboard displays member mentions and ranks, and configured promotion announcements display achievements. Profile and progress command replies are private to the caller. The bot does not sell data or use it for advertising.

## Retention and your choices

Use `/unlink` to stop future checks for that link and clear its active quest. **Unlinking does not delete all your data:** earned rank, processed match history, audit records, and some operational records remain. Riot account display information is removed when no other link uses that account. Existing Discord announcements are not automatically deleted.

The current software has no automatic retention deadline. Contact the operator above to request access, correction, or deletion, including retained records. These requests require manual handling. The operator should retain information only as needed to provide the service or meet applicable obligations.

## Changes and contact

Updates will be published here with a revised date. Contact the operator above with questions or data requests. Discord and Riot also maintain their own privacy policies for their services.
